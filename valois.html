What Valois is

Same conviction.
A different tier.

Valois is not a separate company or a spin-off — it's the top tier of Yorkseed itself, built on the same conviction Yorkseed was founded on: that the most consequential relationships in global capital happen in small rooms, not large ones.

It is not a platform. Not a club. Not a perk bundle. It is a Circle of people who move capital, broker relationships, and make decisions in rooms most people don't reach. Every Valois member also receives full access to Yorkseed Connect's Capital Circle — the venture intelligence and relationship platform underneath it.

New York
Built here.

Yorkseed was founded in New York City. Valois is its curated upper layer, born from the same conviction that shaped Yorkseed from day one.

How membership works

Referral or direct application.
With reference checks.

Membership is by referral or direct application. All submissions require a reference from an existing Valois member or a Yorkseed network contact, and are subject to a discreet vetting process. Admission is considered case by case — the integrity of the Circle depends on it. There is no fixed timeline; the process moves at Valois's pace.

About This Event

Join Yorkseed for an intimate brunch bringing together with-traction founders, previously funded founders, investors, and select ecosystem leaders from around the world during RAISE Summit Paris.

Designed for meaningful conversations beyond the conference floor, The Capital Table creates space for discussions around fundraising, growth, partnerships, international expansion, emerging opportunities, and the future of venture capital.

This is not a regular networking event. It is a curated gathering for people actively building, expanding, and allocating capital across global markets.

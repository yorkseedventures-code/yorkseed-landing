About the Day

Hosted by Yorkseed, in partnership with The 4IR Group and The Engine Accelerator. A private day for family offices and private capital partners, inside the labs and facilities where Boston's robotics and AI companies are being built.

The 4IR Group brings a select group of family offices, principals, and private capital partners inside the Harvard/MIT Kendall Square deep tech ecosystem for one day. Full agenda and locations shared on registration approval.

Early Access, By Design

The companies in this room are building at the frontier of robotics, AI, and science-driven longevity — most of them before the broader market has found them. The day is structured so principals meet founders directly, early, and in person.

About 4IR Group

4IR Group backs early-stage robotics and AI companies rebuilding how physical things get discovered, made, and deployed — across longevity, advanced manufacturing, and defense-adjacent industries. 4IR Group works with a small number of private capital partners on a direct, deal-by-deal basis.

Direct positions rather than blind pools
Programmatic exposure across a defined thesis, rather than one-off allocations
Every opportunity diligenced to the same standard before it reaches a partner
This day is not a fundraise. No securities are offered or sold at this event.
The Room

Family offices, principals, and private capital partners already active in this space, alongside founders from Boston's seed and later-stage companies. Kept small deliberately — the value of the day is as much who is in it as what is presented.

The Evening

An intimate dinner at a separate location following the day's program. Peer relationships, not a networking floor, with room to push back and go deep. The dinner is personally curated and separately invited — attendance at the daytime program does not confer entry.

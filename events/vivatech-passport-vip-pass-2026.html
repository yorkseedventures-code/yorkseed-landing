About This Event

The Yorkseed VIP Passport is more than a ticket to events — it's your entry point into the Yorkseed ecosystem.

VivaTech week is the context. The real value is what comes after: the relationships built in those rooms, the visibility gained across the network, and the ongoing access to a global community of founders, investors, family offices, corporate innovation teams, and ecosystem partners who are active year-round.

As part of the VIP Pass, you receive three months complimentary access to Yorkseed Connect — our capital network for Founding Members. That means a company profile, the ability to publish opportunities in the Deal Room, exposure to our investor network, and an invitation to our quarterly virtual roundtable sessions where members and selected investors come together for discussion, introductions, and ecosystem updates.

We periodically share selected opportunities with our investor network and facilitate introductions where there is mutual interest. The strongest outcomes consistently come from relationships, visibility, and follow-up that continues well beyond a single event week.

The VIP Pass is best understood as access to a broader network and ecosystem — not a single-event product. VivaTech is where it starts.

The right rooms. The right people. The right tools — for every step of the journey, including the ones you have not thought about yet.

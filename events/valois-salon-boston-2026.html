The Salon

Hosted by The 4IR Group, in partnership with Yorkseed. Following The 4IR Group's Investor Day at The Engine, Valois convenes an intimate dinner for the people shaping capital flows in frontier technology.

An intimate gathering of 15–25 family offices, general partners, limited partners, and institutional allocators — the decision-makers responsible for deploying capital across deep tech and physical AI.

The conversation centers on where capital is actually flowing in the frontier: how venture hands off to private capital, how sophisticated investors are underwriting robotics and physical AI, and which opportunities are worth watching before the market catches on.

Joe Collura, founder of The 4IR Group and SECO Bio, joins as a peer — building from The Engine at MIT with a firsthand view of the space. No stage, no deck. Just candid conversation among people who understand the frontier.

An intimate dinner built on discretion, trust, and peer relationships. Not a networking floor. Not a pitch. Meaningful dialogue among allocators.

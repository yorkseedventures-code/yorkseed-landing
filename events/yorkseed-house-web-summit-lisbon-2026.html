About Yorkseed House

The room between the sessions. Yorkseed House is our pop-up home base in Lisbon for the whole of Web Summit week, a private space for founders, investors and operators to work, meet and keep the conversations going between sessions.

Drop in any day without a check-in: cowork, take a meeting, or book a private office or meeting room at reception. Guests also get a discount at the on-site restaurant and bar all week.

The House hosts Yorkseed's curated programming through the week, from community breakfasts and VC office hours to founder and investor meetups, an opening reception with Pitch Roulette, a Capital Table Lunch and invitation-only Valois gatherings.

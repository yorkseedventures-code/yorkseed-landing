What's the Ugly Truth

Beauty tech is booming, but growth has made the category louder, not clearer. AI diagnostics, AR try-ons, personalization, and smart devices are pulling more founders into the space — but more innovation also means more hype, more copycats, and more brands fighting for the same customer attention.

For founders, a strong product is just the entry fee. The real edge comes from knowing what actually drives trust, repeat purchase, and loyalty in a market where branding and novelty can fake momentum. This room is for people who want to separate signal from noise and figure out which trends are truly shaping the future of beauty tech.

Taking the Stage
Zehra Soysal
Founder & CEO · Savanah.ai
Zehra's career began at Google in business strategy, followed by impactful roles at BCG across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. She played a key role in establishing a tech joint venture with Turkey's largest conglomerate. After earning her MBA from Harvard Business School in 2022, she joined Metaphysic.ai in New York, helping scale operations through its $1.4B acquisition. She is now the founder and CEO of Savanah.ai — a visual AI platform helping brands create e-commerce imagery at scale and optimize conversion.
Bonnie Young
Founder & Chief Creative · BY. Bonnie Young · CFDA Member · 2025 FGI RTW Award
Bonnie Young is founder of the luxury brand BY. Bonnie Young, known for timeless, artful design that merges modern couture with effortless ease. She began her career at Ralph Lauren and Donna Karan before launching her own label. Her work has appeared in Vogue, Forbes, and the New York Times, and has been worn by celebrities and Middle Eastern royalty. Young has collaborated with Adidas, Swarovski, and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2. She is also an author, photographer, philanthropist, CFDA member, and recipient of the 2025 FGI Ready-to-Wear Award for excellence.
Yarden Horwitz
Co-Founder · Spate · Ex-Google
Yarden Horwitz is the co-founder of Spate, an AI-driven platform used by 250+ beauty brands including Estée Lauder, L'Oréal, and P&G to make data-driven decisions. Before founding Spate in 2018, Yarden worked at Google, launching the first Google Trends Report and co-founding the trendspotting division. She's been named a top female leader by Entrepreneur Magazine and sits on the boards of CEW and NewBeauty Brain Trust. Her work has been featured in WWD, Vogue Business, and the New York Times.
Jessica Sophia Wong
Moderator · Founder & CEO · Yorkseed
Jessica Sophia Wong is the founder of Yorkseed — a venture intelligence and relationship platform built for the people who move capital and companies across borders. Through Yorkseed Connect, she has built one of the most active cross-border founder and investor ecosystems in the world, operating across 50+ city hubs and running alongside SXSW, Web Summit, Davos, CES, VivaTech, and New York Tech Week. She also founded Valois, a private invitation-only network for family offices, HNWI, and fund managers. Venture Partner at VNTR Capital. Based in NYC and Paris.

About This Event

Join founders, investors, operators, and ecosystem builders for an evening of meaningful conversations above the New York City skyline.

Yorkseed Venture Social is a curated gathering designed to bring together ambitious founders and active investors in a relaxed, high-quality setting. Whether you're building, investing, fundraising, exploring partnerships, or simply looking to expand your network, this is an opportunity to connect with people shaping the future of innovation.

Unlike traditional networking events, Yorkseed gatherings focus on authentic conversations and long-term relationships. Expect a diverse mix of founders, angel investors, venture capitalists, family office representatives, and ecosystem leaders from New York and beyond.

Approval Policy

This is an application-based event. Tickets are role-specific — active building founders with a viable product, actively deploying capital investors, and decision makers. Submissions that do not align with your stated role will not be approved for entry.

You handle your own drinks (drinks are not included). We handle the room. 🥂

Attendee Directory

All registered attendees receive access to a shared directory with names and LinkedIn profiles of fellow participants to help foster meaningful post-event connections. Sharing additional details (current ventures, contact info) is voluntary.

For privacy, the directory link is removed 48 hours after the event.

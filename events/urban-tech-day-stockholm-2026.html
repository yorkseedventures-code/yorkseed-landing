About the Event

We're living in a world of cities: 55% of the world's population now live in cities, rising to 68% by 2050 (UN). In Sweden that figure is already 88% (SCB).

Cities are complex systems that need cross-disciplinary expertise working together to become more resilient and sustainable. Urban Tech Day is an invitation to everyone whose work touches SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities — to meet fellow citymakers, whatever vertical or industry they work in.

This event is for investors, founders, and ecosystem players interested in ClimateTech, ConstructionTech, CivicTech, EdTech, FitnessTech, HealthTech, MobilityTech, PropTech, QueerTech, ResilienceTech, SportsTech, TourismTech, and the many other Techs helping cities become better places to live.

Elevate Recovery Lounge at Yorkseed House

📅 Thursday, June 4, 2026 · 9:00 AM–5:00 PM EDT
📍 Belong NYC · 218 Lafayette St, SoHo
About This Event

As part of Yorkseed House during New York Tech Week 2026, Elevate is taking over the space for a full-day wellness and recovery pop-up designed for founders, investors, operators, and ecosystem leaders who need a moment to recharge during a packed week.

Throughout the day, guests experience 15-minute recovery sessions featuring zero gravity recovery chairs, compression boot sessions, red light therapy, and eye massage masks.

Whether you are between meetings, coming from another event, or simply looking for a place to reset — this is an opportunity to slow down, recover, and reconnect in a more intentional environment during the chaos of Tech Week.

Sessions run throughout the day: Morning (9–11am), Midday (11am–1pm), Afternoon (1–3pm), Late Afternoon (3–5pm).

A $10 contribution is required at the door via Venmo or Stripe. This is a separate registration from the main Yorkseed House RSVP.

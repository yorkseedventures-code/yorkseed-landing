Questions

Partner FAQ

What does the ▲ mean on The Yorkseed List?

It marks events from Yorkseed community partners. Partner events show a “▲ Yorkseed partner” badge in the list, and cities with partner events show a ▲ on the map.

Who can become a community partner?

Communities, event organizers, conferences, accelerators, venues and ecosystem programs whose events bring founders, investors and builders together. Yorkseed is sector agnostic, except cannabis and casino gambling.

What's the difference between a community partner and a co-host?

Community partners add Yorkseed's logo and a short line to their event. Co-hosts also list Yorkseed as a visible co-host on the event page, so Yorkseed can invite its network directly.

I want Yorkseed to build the event for us.

That's Host with Yorkseed: private lunches, dinners and side events where you host and Yorkseed builds the room.

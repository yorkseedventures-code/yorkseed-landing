How the portfolio grows

Different companies. One shared advantage.

Yorkseed does not force every company into the same thesis, visual identity or capital path. The common thread is leverage: a problem we understand, access to the people who can validate it, and an ecosystem that can help the company find its first customers, partners, operators and investors.

Building something that belongs here?

We work with a small number of founders, operators and early products where Yorkseed can materially change the trajectory of the company.

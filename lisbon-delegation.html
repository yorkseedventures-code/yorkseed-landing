Web Summit is enormous.
Go in with people.

We go to Web Summit every year. The founders who get the most out of Lisbon aren't the ones with the biggest booth. They're the ones who arrive with people already in their corner.

This year we're bringing a small group of founders with us. Where there's a real fit, we'll try to introduce you to investors and partners from the Yorkseed network. We'll also give you a group to walk into the week with, and stay in touch after everyone flies home.

You don't need a Web Summit ticket to be part of it. Not sure you want to buy one? Our famous master side event spreadsheet has everything happening around Lisbon that week. We might add more programs along the way, and everyone will be in one group chat so nobody misses a thing.

Spots are limited so we can make the introductions ourselves. We can't promise any specific meeting or outcome, but we'll do our best to connect you with the right people.

Questions

Before you apply

Is the Web Summit ticket included? Do I need one?

It isn't included, and you don't need one to join. If you want to go inside Web Summit, buy it through Web Summit. If you're still deciding, the delegation works either way, and our famous master side event spreadsheet lists the side events happening all week.

What is KoiCard, and how do I get access?

KoiCard is an app that reads business cards and QR codes and saves them as contacts, with notes and color labels, so you can follow up after Web Summit. Every delegate gets free access for two weeks, with 300 scans, from November 5 to November 18. Once you've joined, we'll send you an access code. Sign up at koicard.app, open Settings and enter the code.

Can I advertise or get editorial coverage in the Deal Book?

Yes. The Deal Book is distributed digitally across the Yorkseed network and our channels. Companies, funds and partners can buy ad placements or paid editorial features, in the Lisbon Deal Book and in the annual Yorkseed Delegation edition. Paid editorial is labeled as sponsored. Write to partnership@yorkseed.co for options and rates.

Will you feature me publicly?

Only if you say yes. The application asks whether we can feature you, and if you agree, you send us a link to your headshot and your company or firm logo. We may use your name, role, company, a short description and those images in the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation press release and on Yorkseed's social channels. This covers founders and investors. If you say no, nothing changes for the rest of the delegation.

Will there be more programs?

We might add more programs to the delegation. Everyone will be in one WhatsApp group chat, so you'll hear about anything new there.

Why per company?

Most of what you get goes to the company: the Deal Book profile, introductions, the showcase and Yorkseed Connect. One person is included, and extra teammates are €175 each.

When do I pay?

At checkout, right after the form. Payment runs through Stripe. We review every delegate, and if it's not a fit we refund you in full within 48 hours. Once you've paid, we add you to the delegation's Luma guest list, where you'll get venue details and updates for the week.

Am I guaranteed a showcase slot?

No. Every founder in the delegation is considered and we pick the companies that fit the investors joining. Premium companies go first.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if you cancel before October 30. After that your profile and introduction work are already in motion, so it's non-refundable.

I'm an investor. Can I join?

Please do. It's complimentary for investors, LPs and family offices. Choose "Investor" at the top of the form. If you can only make the virtual showcase, request a seat on Luma.

Web Summit is enormous.
Go in with people.

We go to Web Summit every year. The founders who get the most out of Lisbon aren't the ones with the biggest booth. They're the ones who arrive with people already in their corner.

This year we're bringing founders and investors with us. Where there's a real fit, we'll try to introduce you to investors and partners from the Yorkseed network. We'll also give you a group to walk into the week with, and stay in touch after everyone flies home.

You don't need a Web Summit ticket to be part of it. Not sure you want to buy one? Our famous master side event list has everything happening around Lisbon that week. We might add more programs along the way, and everyone will be in one group chat so nobody misses a thing.

Spots are limited so we can make the introductions ourselves. We can't promise any specific meeting or outcome, but we'll do our best to connect you with the right people.

Questions

Before you apply

Is the Web Summit ticket included? Do I need one?

It isn't included, and you don't need one to join. If you want to go inside Web Summit, buy it through Web Summit. If you're still deciding, the delegation works either way, and our famous master side event list lists the side events happening all week.

What is KoiCard, and how do I get access?

KoiCard is an app that reads business cards and QR codes and saves them as contacts, with notes and color labels, so you can follow up after Web Summit. Every delegate gets free access for two weeks, with 300 scans, from November 5 to November 18. Once you've joined, we'll send you an access code. Sign up at koicard.app, open Settings and enter the code.

Can I advertise or get editorial coverage in the Deal Book?

Yes. The Deal Book is distributed digitally across the Yorkseed network and our channels. Companies, funds and partners can take a partner page in the Lisbon Deal Book, in The Yorkseed Annual at year end, or both. Partner pages are labelled Partner. See packages and rates, or write to partnership@yorkseed.co.

What do I need for my Deal Book page?

If you say yes on the application, we ask for a tagline, two or three sentences about your company or firm, three keywords, your website, links to your headshot and logo, and where you'd like the QR code on your page to send people. You can also add a headshot link for each teammate. We'll build the page from that. Please make sure anyone with a photo link can open it.

Will you feature me publicly?

Only if you say yes. The application asks whether we can feature you, and if you agree, you send us a link to your headshot and your company or firm logo. We may use your name, role, company, a short description and those images in the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation press release and on Yorkseed's social channels. This covers founders and investors. If you say no, nothing changes for the rest of the delegation.

Will there be more programs?

We might add more programs to the delegation. Everyone will be in one WhatsApp group chat, so you'll hear about anything new there.

I can't make it to Lisbon. Can I still be in the Deal Book?

Yes. Choose Founder Deal Book Virtual, €195 per company. You get your own Deal Book page, shared across the Yorkseed network during Web Summit week and online after, consideration for the December Global Founder Showcase and one month of Yorkseed Connect. Virtual teammates are €95 each. It doesn't include Yorkseed House, the kickoff, meetups, KoiCard or the delegate group. Applications and profiles close November 11.

Why per company?

Most of what you get goes to the company: the Deal Book profile, introductions, the showcase and Yorkseed Connect. One person is included, and extra teammates are €175 each.

When do I pay?

Right after you apply. Founders and partners get a payment button as soon as the application is sent, which locks the price. We review every application and confirm your place within two days, and investors get a confirmation instead of a payment. Once you've paid and are confirmed, we add you to the delegation's Luma guest list, where you'll get venue details and updates for the week.

Am I guaranteed a showcase slot?

No. Every founder in the delegation is considered and we pick the companies that fit the investors joining.

I'm an executive or a service provider. Can I join?

Yes, as a Partner Delegate, €995 per company (€795 until October 29, €895 until November 4). That covers executives, corporates, advisors, law firms, banks, recruiters, EOR and payroll companies, consultants and other ecosystem organizations. You get a seat at the Executive Lunch on Monday, November 9, a clearly labeled Partner profile in the Deal Book, access to the delegate group and selected gatherings, three months of Yorkseed Connect and relevant connection opportunities. We ask every partner to offer delegates a useful perk, like a free consultation, a discount or credits. Your perk is also listed in the Vault on Yorkseed Connect. Extra teammates are €175 each, and each one gets an Executive Lunch seat too. Choose "Partner" at the top of the form.

I'm an investor. Can I join?

Please do. It's complimentary for VCs, angel groups, LPs, family offices and other allocators, subject to approval. We run due diligence on every investor and approve VCs, angel groups and networks, LPs, family offices and other allocators only. If you raise for others, offer market entry, or are looking for new clients, join as a Partner Delegate. Choose "Investor" at the top of the form. If you can only make the virtual showcase, request a seat on Luma.

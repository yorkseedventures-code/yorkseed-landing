Questions

Before you apply

Is the Web Summit ticket included?

No. Buy it through Web Summit. If you don't have one yet you can still apply, just make sure you have it before Lisbon.

Why per company?

Most of what you get goes to the company: the magazine profile, introductions, the showcase and Yorkseed Connect. One person is included, and extra teammates are €175 each.

When do I pay?

Request your ticket on Luma. I review every request and reply within two days. You're only charged once you're approved.

Am I guaranteed a showcase slot?

No. Every founder in the delegation is considered and I pick the companies that fit the investors joining. Premium companies go first.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, if you cancel before October 30. After that your profile and introductions are already in motion, so it's non-refundable.

I'm an investor. Can I join?

Please do. It's complimentary for investors, LPs and family offices. Request the Investor / LP / Family Office ticket on Luma. If you can only make the virtual showcase, request a seat on Luma.

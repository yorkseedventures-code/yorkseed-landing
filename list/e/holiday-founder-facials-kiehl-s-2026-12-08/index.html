Join us for an interactive practical discussion on the legal considerations every founder should understand before raising capital. Keynote Colin Kirby, Foley Hoag, Emerging Companies and Venture Capital Fochair Colin will walk founders through the most important legal provisions in early-stage financing documents, explaining what they mean in practice, which terms deserve closer attention, and how founders can avoid common mistakes before they become costly.

SWITCH Singapore 2026 side events

SWITCH (Singapore Week of Innovation and Technology) is Asia's innovation festival, organized by Enterprise Singapore with support from the National Research Foundation. Its flagship is the SLINGSHOT startup pitching finals, alongside keynotes, market access sessions and roundtables on AI, climate and energy, quantum, advanced materials, healthcare and biotech, and digital connectivity. The 2025 edition drew over 25,000 attendees from 100+ markets. This is the master side event list for SWITCH Singapore, October 26 to 29, 2026.

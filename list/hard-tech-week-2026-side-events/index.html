Hard Tech Week 2026 side events

Hard Tech Week is Utah's physical-technology week, run by 47G with Utah Tech Week, Nucleus and Silicon Slopes. It covers aerospace, defense, drones, advanced manufacturing, energy and space, with a hackathon, factory tours and the Zero Gravity Summit at the Salt Palace on Oct 28-29.

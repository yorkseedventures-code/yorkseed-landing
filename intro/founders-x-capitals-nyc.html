About tonight's host

Yorkseed

Yorkseed is a global capital network and venture infrastructure platform connecting founders, investors, fund managers, capital allocators and ecosystem leaders, from early stage to IPO. With 50+ city hubs across five continents, we bring together curated gatherings, global delegations and strategic relationships that turn connections into meaningful opportunities.

Beyond events, Yorkseed Connect is our intelligent dealmaking platform for investor discovery, warm introductions, fund diligence, strategic partnerships and deal flow. Valois by Yorkseed is our dedicated capital circle connecting family offices, LPs, GPs and institutional investors. Across our ecosystem, we focus on quality over quantity, bringing the right people together and creating opportunities that extend well beyond the room.

Join our Hong Kong delegation to connect with global investors, founders, family offices and business leaders, with access to AFF’s dealmaking platform to discover opportunities and arrange meetings ahead of the forum.

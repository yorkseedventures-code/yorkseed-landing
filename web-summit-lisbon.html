Web Summit Lisbon

What is Yorkseed doing at Web Summit Lisbon 2026?

Yorkseed is running a week of founder and investor programming around Web Summit Lisbon, November 8 to 13, 2026: Yorkseed House Lisbon, open all week from November 9, an Executive Lunch on November 9, The Yorkseed Soirée – Web Summit Opening Night 2026 that evening, a Capital Lunch on November 10, an Allocator Lunch on November 11, and the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation for founders.

Do I need a Web Summit ticket to join Yorkseed events in Lisbon?

No. Yorkseed events and the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation do not require a Web Summit ticket. If you want to go inside Web Summit itself, buy a ticket through Web Summit. Yorkseed also keeps a master side event spreadsheet listing events happening around Lisbon that week.

What is the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation?

Founders and investors travelling to Web Summit Lisbon together with Yorkseed. Delegates may get introductions to investors and partners where there is a fit, a kickoff on November 9, meetups through the week, a delegate group chat, Yorkseed House access throughout the week with the option to host a small event or office hour there, a profile in the delegation Deal Book, consideration for a virtual December founder showcase and six months of Yorkseed Connect. Introductions are not guaranteed. Pricing starts at €495 per company, and investors, LPs and family offices join free.

Who is the delegation for?

Founders who will be in Lisbon for Web Summit week, building something venture-scale from pre-seed to Series A, raising now or within the next year. Companies in cannabis or casino gambling are not accepted.

Will Yorkseed add more programs?

Possibly. Yorkseed might add more programs to the delegation, and every delegate is in one group chat so updates reach everyone.

Where are the Yorkseed Web Summit events held?

Venues in Lisbon are shared with approved attendees before the events. Applications and RSVPs run through Luma.

Storm-Cancelled Conference Day Becomes Investor Pop-Up: WET SUMMIT Lisbon 2026 × Valois by Yorkseed Convenes Allocators in Under 24 Hours

November 11, 2026 Lisbon, Portugal

LISBON, Portugal: November 11, 2026 When Web Summit organizers announced that Day 2 of the conference would be cancelled due to Storm Charles, a few members of the investment community decided not to let the week's momentum disappear. By the next afternoon, WET SUMMIT Lisbon 2026, an investor-only pop-up summit and unconference (wetsummitlisbon.com), was underway in Lisbon in partnership with Valois by Yorkseed.

From a midnight idea to a room full of investors

The idea came from Gergő Gulyás, supported by Serkan Kurtuluş. Ashley Martis of StartupFuel began looking for a venue, and Gonçalo arranged Garagem da Vizinha by WOTELS within about ten minutes. Ricardo suggested the name WET SUMMIT, Susie Trujillo of STRUC VENTURES set up the Luma registration page, and Serkan, working with AI agents, built the website. The group went from idea to an open event in under 90 minutes, and about 70 people registered in the first 15.

Around midnight, Susie and Ashley shared what they had already put together with Jessica Sophia Wong, founder of Yorkseed, who was hosting a Yorkseed event at Bar Na Colina. Planning began on the spot. The teams agreed to fold the Allocator Table, Valois by Yorkseed's originally scheduled private evening for capital allocators, into WET SUMMIT, combining two communities into one gathering.

Curated, not crowded

WET SUMMIT took place on Wednesday, November 11, at Garagem da Vizinha by WOTELS in Saldanha. The organizers stayed up all night, with each team going through its own registration list. The Valois team reviewed applications, verified investor credentials and conducted due diligence to curate a room of active investors and capital allocators, including family offices, LPs, GPs, institutional investors, venture capitalists and private capital leaders.

The day followed an unconference format, with a topic market, three discussion rounds and group read-outs. With Web Summit directing investors toward the pop-up, interest continued to build.

"The goal wasn't simply to gather as many people as possible. It was to create an environment where meaningful conversations could lead to partnerships, co-investments and lasting relationships."

Jessica Sophia Wong, Founder, Yorkseed

After party, planned in 30 minutes

The evening continued with THE NIGHTCAP: Lisbon Edition, a WET SUMMIT × Valois after party at one of Lisbon's top cocktail bars, put together in roughly 30 minutes of planning. Valois is normally reserved for allocators; for this one night, the door opened to founders as well, giving the room a chance to keep talking after the sessions closed.

A story of its own

By the time Web Summit resumed for its final conference day, WET SUMMIT had become a talking point across the conference, including among people who had not attended. PÚBLICO, one of Portugal's leading newspapers, reported on WET SUMMIT the same week.

"No single person made it happen. It took people with different strengths, networks and resources coming together, trusting one another and figuring things out as we went. The value of a network isn't measured by how many people you know, but by what people can accomplish when they come together."

Jessica Sophia Wong, Founder, Yorkseed

A huge thank you to Susie, Ashley, Gergő, Serkan and everyone behind the scenes.

About WET SUMMIT

WET SUMMIT Lisbon 2026 was an independent, investor-only pop-up summit and unconference, created in response to the cancellation of a Web Summit conference day. It is not affiliated with Web Summit. wetsummitlisbon.com

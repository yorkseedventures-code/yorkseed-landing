Yorkseed Launches Its Lisbon Delegation at Web Summit and Will Lead a Delegation Back to the Asian Financial Forum in Hong Kong in 2027

October 21, 2026 New York, NY

NEW YORK, October 21, 2026: Yorkseed, the curated global venture network for founders and investors, today launched the Yorkseed Lisbon Delegation: founders and investors travelling together to Web Summit Lisbon, November 8 to 12, 2026. Yorkseed also announced that it will return to the Asian Financial Forum (AFF) in Hong Kong on January 26 and 27, 2027, leading another Yorkseed Delegation to Asia's flagship finance and investment conference.

Both delegations follow the same model: founders, investors and partners from Yorkseed's 50+ city hubs arrive together, are introduced to one another where there is a real fit, and stay connected after the week is over. Every delegate has a profile in a published Deal Book, and anyone can request an introduction through Yorkseed.

The Lisbon Delegation

The delegation kicks off in Lisbon on Sunday, November 8, the day before Web Summit opens. Delegates receive:

  • Introductions to investors and partners from the Yorkseed network, made personally by the Yorkseed team where stage and sector fit.
  • Gatherings: the November 8 kickoff, Yorkseed meetups through the week and a private group for delegates.
  • A page in the Lisbon Deal Book, organized into Companies, Partners and Investors, shared across the Yorkseed network during Web Summit week and kept online afterwards. Introduction requests are open from November 1 to 21.
  • The Global Founder Showcase, a virtual session in December where selected founders present to invited investors, including investors who were not in Lisbon.
  • Six months of Yorkseed Connect, Yorkseed's member platform for discovery, diligence and introductions.

The founding rate for the first ten companies has sold out. Applications remain open to founders at €595 per company, and investors, LPs and family offices join complimentary, subject to approval. A Web Summit ticket is not required. Applications are reviewed at yorkseed.co/lisbon-delegation, and the Lisbon Deal Book is available to read online.

A week of programming around Web Summit

Alongside the delegation, Yorkseed is hosting a series of side events around Web Summit Lisbon, its third year of programming during the conference:

  • November 8: Yorkseed Summit Closing and Web Summit Opening Night
  • November 9: Yorkseed Executive Lunch
  • November 10: Yorkseed Capital Lunch
  • November 11: Yorkseed Allocator Lunch

"Conferences like Web Summit are enormous. The founders who get the most out of the week are the ones who arrive with people already in their corner. Lisbon is where we bring that to our whole network, and Hong Kong is where we take it next."

Jessica Sophia Wong, Founder, Yorkseed

Returning to the Asian Financial Forum, January 26 and 27, 2027

On January 26 and 27, 2027, Yorkseed will return to the Asian Financial Forum in Hong Kong, organized by the Hong Kong Trade Development Council at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, leading another Yorkseed Delegation. The 2027 forum is its 20th edition.

Yorkseed's relationship with AFF runs deep. Yorkseed has been an Official Community Partner of the forum, and founder Jessica Sophia Wong served as Mission Leader for the French Delegation at the 18th Asian Financial Forum, where she was featured in a China Daily video interview. The 2027 delegation will bring founders, investors and allocators from Yorkseed's hubs to Hong Kong, with a dedicated Deal Book and introductions through the week.

Yorkseed Connect members hear about new delegations first. Founders, investors and partners can register interest at yorkseed.co/delegation.

The Yorkseed Annual

At the end of each year, the Deal Books from every Yorkseed Delegation are brought together in one edition, The Yorkseed Annual: every delegation and every delegate from the year, in one place. The 2026 edition will include the Lisbon Delegation. A limited number of partner pages are available.

A busy year for Yorkseed

  • The Yorkseed List: Yorkseed's global calendar of startup, founder and investor events across 50+ cities, including the side events around major tech weeks and conferences, is now live at yorkseed.co/list. Listing an event is free.
  • Valois: in July, Yorkseed launched Valois, its invitation-only circle for family offices, fund managers and allocators. Its first activation, Valois at the Louvre, took place during RAISE Summit Paris.

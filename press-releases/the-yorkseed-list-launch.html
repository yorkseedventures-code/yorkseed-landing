Yorkseed Launches The Yorkseed List, a Free Global Calendar of Startup, Tech and Investor Events in 50+ Cities

October 21, 2026 New York, NY

NEW YORK, October 21, 2026: Yorkseed, the curated global venture network for founders and investors, today launched The Yorkseed List, a free global calendar of startup, tech, founder and investor events. At launch, the List tracks 467 upcoming events across 44 cities, along with the side events around the year's major tech weeks and conferences.

The List started where Yorkseed did. In 2023, Yorkseed's founder shared a curated spreadsheet of side events during SXSW Austin, and it spread across the startup ecosystem. Every year since, founders and investors have asked for the same thing in more cities: one trusted place to see which events are worth going to. The Yorkseed List is that answer, built for every city in the Yorkseed network.

What the List includes

  • Every city in one place: founder meetups, investor dinners, pitch nights, demo days, VC office hours, conferences and community events, organized by date and local time, from New York and San Francisco to London, Singapore, Lisbon and Bengaluru.
  • Conference side events: the master side event list for the major tech weeks and conferences, including Web Summit Lisbon and Slush Helsinki.
  • A page for every city: each city has its own page, for example New York, San Francisco Bay Area, London and Singapore, plus a directory of all cities.
  • Your city first: the List opens on your home city, based on your time zone, and lets you save events to plan your week.
  • A map: every city on the Yorkseed map, with the community partners and venues behind the events.
  • The weekly email: the best events in your city every Monday, through Yorkseed Insider.

Free to list, reviewed by people

Organizers can add an event for free by pasting a Luma, Partiful or Eventbrite link. Every submission is reviewed by the Yorkseed team within a day, which keeps the List focused on events with a clear purpose for founders, investors and builders. Community partners are marked on the List and the map, and organizers can request a co-hosting or community partnership with Yorkseed directly from the List.

"The first list I made was a spreadsheet for one week in Austin. Three years later, people still ask us where they should be every night of a big week. The Yorkseed List is the grown-up version of that spreadsheet, for every city we're in and every week that matters."

Jessica Sophia Wong, Founder, Yorkseed

For brands, venues and organizers

Brands, venues and organizers can reach the List's audience through placements across the homepage, city pages, the map and the event list, with an optional page in Yorkseed's Deal Book. Placements follow the same review as events. Details are available from info@yorkseed.co.

The Yorkseed List is live now at yorkseed.co/list.

Questions

Before you apply

Does it cost anything to join?

No. The delegation is free and you receive a complimentary AFF delegate pass. You pay for your own flights. Hotel is included only if you choose the full bundle. The extras on this page are optional.

Do I need to buy an AFF ticket?

No. A full AFF pass usually costs over $1,000, but as official community partner of HKTDC, Yorkseed gives every delegate a complimentary AFF delegate pass, with onsite access to the full 2-day program, the exhibition areas and the AFF Deal-Making platform, plus keynote luncheons (meal entitlements are subject to prior reservation). AFF has its own short registration form for each delegate pass, and we'll send it to you once your place is confirmed.

What's the difference between my standard page and the editorial page?

Every delegate can have a standard profile page in the Deal Book, free. It says who you are: what you do, a few keywords, your photo and logo, and a QR code to connect. The editorial page is one additional full page for what you want people to know or think about: your story, a company announcement, a fundraising or expansion update, why Hong Kong or Asia, or your view on your industry. You send the content and we review it lightly. It's labeled "Contributed by" your company and isn't a plain advertisement.

What does the hotel in the Full Bundle include?

Three nights, January 25 to 28, 2027, in one room for one delegate at a hotel in Wan Chai. Rooms are limited and are confirmed once your place is, on a first-come basis among those who choose the full bundle. We confirm the hotel name and booking details when we send your payment link. Hotel isn't sold on its own. If you'd rather choose your own hotel, you can book any Wan Chai hotel directly, and we're happy to share suggestions.

What does the coworking pass include?

Four days of coworking access in Hong Kong during AFF week, so you have somewhere to work, take calls and hold meetings between sessions and side events. Wi-Fi and power are included. It's sold only in the Editorial + Coworking and Full Bundle options.

When do I pay for an add-on?

Whenever you like: use the buy button on the Editorial Feature or Editorial + Coworking, and pay securely by card. You don't have to wait for your place to be confirmed, and joining is free either way. The Full Bundle is reserved by email because rooms are limited. The shared booth is charged only once at least 7 delegates have committed.

Can I exhibit at AFF?

Yes, it's optional. The interest form asks whether you only want to join as a delegate, would like to share a booth with the Yorkseed Delegation, or would like your own booth. The shared booth runs for two days, January 26 and 27, at $799 per person. You pay nothing now: you just tell us you're interested. We charge only once at least 7 delegates have committed, and if we don't reach 7, there's no shared booth. We'll confirm once we know, and send payment details to those who are in. If you'd rather have your own booth, you can do that too. Choose it on the interest form and we'll send you the options.

Can I take part without going to Hong Kong?

Yes, as a Virtual Delegate for $149, though we'd love to see you in Hong Kong, where the delegation is free. A Virtual Delegate gets a profile page in the Yorkseed Hong Kong Delegation Deal Book, with a link to share, and nothing else: no AFF pass, no onsite access, and no place in the delegate group chat. Places are limited to 20 and each application is reviewed. Profiles are due January 15. Virtual Delegates can also buy the Editorial Feature ($295), which is open to in-person and virtual delegates alike. The coworking pass, hotel and shared booth are for people attending in person. If you decide to attend in person, join the free delegation instead. Become a Virtual Delegate

Can I advertise or sponsor the Deal Book?

Yes. The Deal Book is distributed digitally across the Yorkseed network and our channels. Companies, funds and partners can buy ad placements or sponsored sections. Write to partnership@yorkseed.co for options and rates.

What do I need for my Deal Book page?

If you say yes on the detailed application, we ask for a tagline, two or three sentences about your company or firm, three keywords, your website, links to your headshot and logo, and where you'd like the QR code on your page to send people. We'll build the page from that. Please make sure anyone with a photo link can open it. You can see the Lisbon Deal Book from our previous delegation, or a sample page for this one.

Will you feature me publicly?

Only if you say yes. The application asks whether we can feature you, and if you agree, you send us a link to your headshot and your company or firm logo. We may use your name, role, company, a short description and those images in the Yorkseed Hong Kong Delegation press release and on Yorkseed's social channels. This covers founders and investors. If you say no, nothing changes for the rest of the delegation.

I'm an investor. Can I join?

Please do. It's free for investors, LPs and family offices. Choose "Investor" at the top of the form.

How fund managers raise a fund

Most first and emerging managers lose months chasing the wrong LPs. Work the list in this order.

  1. Build your target listFilter this list by region and type, then open each LP to see what it has backed and when.
  2. Check the fitMatch your strategy and fund size to the managers each LP has committed to. First-time and emerging manager programs are tagged.
  3. Research each LPRun Fund Diligence on any allocator inside Connect before you reach out. Open Connect
  4. Set your termsDraft and compare fund terms, then estimate set up and running costs. Fund Terms Builder
  5. Get warm pathsRequest intros through the network. The other member decides, so no cold outreach.
  6. Meet LPs in personAllocator lunches and city events bring managers and LPs into one room. See events

Where can I find LPs for my first fund?

Start with fund of funds, emerging-manager programs and family offices. Filter by type above, then open each one to see the managers it has backed and the public source.

Does a listing mean an LP will back my fund?

No. Listing shows who commits capital to funds and where we found it. It is not a promise to back you, and not every LP accepts new managers.

Where does the commitment data come from?

Public sources such as LP annual reports, board papers and official announcements. Every commitment links to its source and carries a date.

Can an LP fix or claim its listing?

Yes. Staff at a listed allocator can claim the profile for free, or ask us to correct or remove an entry at info@yorkseed.co.

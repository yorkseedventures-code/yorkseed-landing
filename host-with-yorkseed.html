Mission aligned only

We say no more often than we say yes.

Yorkseed is built on intentional networking. Every hosted event has to strengthen the network our members trust, so we only take on events that meet these standards.

  • Built for founders and investors. A clear business purpose, with active founders, investors or allocators at the table.
  • Intentional, not open. No general parties, open mixers or events anyone can walk into.
  • Conversation first. No hard selling or pitches at the table.
  • Sector exclusions. No cannabis or casino gambling.
  • Co-branded with Yorkseed. Every hosted event follows the Yorkseed branding guidelines.

Questions

Before you request

How much does it cost?

Every event is different. Pricing depends on format, city, guest count and venue. Send a request and we will reply with a tailored proposal.

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend at least four weeks for lunches and dinners, and longer for major conference weeks, when the best venues and guests book early.

Which cities do you cover?

Any city with a Yorkseed hub, across 50+ cities on five continents, plus major conference weeks worldwide.

Who will be in the room?

Founders, investors, allocators and executives from the Yorkseed network, matched to your goals. We agree on the target guest profile with you before invitations go out.

Whose brand is on the event?

Yours, alongside Yorkseed. Every hosted event follows the Yorkseed branding guidelines.

What if we don't have a budget?

Hosted events are paid engagements, so we need a budget to build one for you. If you are running your own event, Yorkseed can still join as a Community Partner or Co-host and bring it to our network. Request an event partnership.

Can you turn down a request?

Yes. We only take on events that are mission aligned and built for founders and investors. Cannabis and casino gambling events are not accepted.

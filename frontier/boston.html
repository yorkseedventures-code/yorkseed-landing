001/ A day inside the frontier

Where research becomes companies, and companies get funded.

Frontier Day brings together the people building, funding, and commercializing technologies moving beyond the screen and into the physical world: selected founders, investors, researchers, and industry leaders across Boston and Cambridge, for one immersive day.

This isn't a conference in the traditional sense. Participants move through the city's innovation landscape rather than sitting in one room all day.

002/ Inside frontier day

Five categories, one room.

Hard-technology companies where the science itself is the moat, long development cycles, real IP, and a path to categories that don't exist yet.

  • Research advantage & defensible IP
  • University and lab spinouts
  • Capital-intensive technology roadmaps
  • Lab-to-market commercialization

AI moving off the screen and into machines and physical environments, where models meet the laws of physics.

  • Vision, language & action models
  • Real-time decision-making & autonomy
  • Manufacturing & healthcare applications
  • Human-machine interaction

Machines operating at scale in real environments, alongside people, with measurable performance.

  • Robotics facility access & live demonstrations
  • Automation for factories, warehouses & infrastructure
  • Integrating robotics into existing operations
  • Commercialization & scaling challenges

Digital-native AI, models, agents, and infrastructure, the layer that increasingly sits underneath physical and deep-tech systems too.

  • Foundation models & applied AI products
  • Agentic systems & developer infrastructure
  • Enterprise & vertical AI applications
  • Where digital AI meets physical systems

Where venture, CVC and private capital are placing bets on frontier technology, and what they need to see beyond the science.

  • What gets funded next
  • Where venture ends and other capital enters
  • Building investable deep-tech companies
  • Financing longer-duration technology cycles

How joining works

Request once. Get verified. Start connecting.

Spam and fake profiles are rising across social platforms, especially on WhatsApp. So invite links are never posted publicly, and every member is checked by hand.

1

Request

Pick your hub and tell us who you are: your role, company and a profile link. It takes two minutes.

2

Get verified

Our team checks every request. Hubs are for active founders, accredited investors and ecosystem builders.

3

Join

Approved members get the invite link directly. Introduce yourself, share what you need, and how you can help.

Join only the hubs where you live or often spend time. City hubs exist for real, in-person relationships, so joining several to collect leads isn’t allowed. Share quality events in your own city, no unsolicited self-promotion, and give as much as you take.
